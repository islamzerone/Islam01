Assalam-o-Alaikum! I'm Ahmad Raza, the developer of this app.

Islam01 brings the Quran (with Urdu translation), the Hadith books, prayer times, Qibla, the Hijri calendar, daily masnoon duas and guides for prayer, wudu and janaza together in one simple place.

My goal is to keep it simple, fast and useful for everyone. If you find a mistake or have a suggestion, please let me know.

Keep me in your prayers. JazakAllah Khair.
